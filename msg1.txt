The street was quiet in the early morning, the kind of quiet that only exists
before the rest of the city wakes up. A single bus passed by, its brakes
squealing as it turned the corner and disappeared. I stood by the window with
a cup of coffee, watching the light change across the buildings. It felt like
time had slowed down, giving me a chance to think about things I usually ignore.
There were messages I never sent, plans I never finished, and conversations
that ended too early. The smell of rain lingered in the air, mixing with dust
and old pavement. Somewhere nearby, a radio played a song I half remembered,
and for a moment everything felt balanced and calm.
