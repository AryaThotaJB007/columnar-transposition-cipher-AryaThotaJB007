I started writing late at night when the room was finally quiet and no one was
expecting anything from me. The cursor blinked on the screen while I tried to
decide where to begin. Words came slowly at first, then faster, filling the
space with thoughts I had been holding onto for weeks. Some of them were messy
and unclear, but that felt honest. I wrote about work, about missed deadlines,
about the strange comfort of routines and the fear of changing them. Outside,
cars passed occasionally, their headlights flashing against the wall. By the
time I finished, the sky was beginning to lighten, and I realized I had been
awake all night without noticing.
