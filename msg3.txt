The song played softly through broken speakers, skipping every few seconds but
never fully stopping. I knew the words well enough to fill in the gaps, humming
along while the beat faded in and out. It reminded me of long drives with the
windows down, the road stretching endlessly ahead. Every chorus felt like a
memory, every verse like a promise that things would eventually make sense.
Even when the music cut out completely, the rhythm stayed with me. I tapped my
fingers on the table, letting the silence carry the rest of the tune forward.
